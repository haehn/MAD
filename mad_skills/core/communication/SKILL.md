# Communication

Use this skill when reporting findings, decisions, instructions, status, or requests.

## Process

1. Identify the audience.
2. Determine what the audience needs to know or do.
3. Lead with the most important information.
4. Use clear structure and precise terminology.
5. Remove irrelevant detail.
6. Make requests and next actions explicit.
7. Preserve uncertainty where it matters.

## Rules

- Prefer concise language.
- Do not bury decisions or blockers.
- Avoid jargon unless appropriate for the audience.
- Do not overstate confidence.

## Output

Produce communication that is:
- clear
- actionable
- audience-appropriate
- concise
