# Research

Use this skill when progress depends on information that is uncertain, external, unfamiliar, or rapidly changing.

## Process

1. Define the exact research question.
2. Identify authoritative sources.
3. Gather information directly relevant to the question.
4. Compare sources where appropriate.
5. Separate verified facts from interpretation.
6. Identify contradictions or missing information.
7. Summarize implications for the project.
8. Recommend further validation when needed.

## Rules

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
- Do not present assumptions as facts.
- Keep research scoped to the decision at hand.
- Note dates, versions, or context when they materially affect conclusions.

## Output

Return:
- conclusion
- key findings
- implications
- uncertainties
- recommendation
- sources
