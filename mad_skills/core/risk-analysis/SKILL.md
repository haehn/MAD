# Risk Analysis

Use this skill during planning, execution, or review when uncertainty may affect outcomes.

## Process

1. Identify possible failure modes or blockers.
2. Estimate likelihood and impact qualitatively.
3. Identify early warning signals.
4. Determine which risks require mitigation.
5. Propose practical mitigation or fallback options.
6. Reassess risks as the project changes.

## Rules

- Focus on material risks.
- Distinguish known issues from hypothetical possibilities.
- Prefer mitigations proportional to the risk.
- Highlight irreversible or high-impact decisions.

## Output

For each important risk provide:
- description
- likelihood
- impact
- mitigation
- contingency
