# Planning

Use this skill when a project or objective must be converted into an actionable plan.

## Process

1. Clarify the desired outcome.
2. Identify constraints, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. Break the work into meaningful phases or tasks.
4. Order work by dependency.
5. Identify what can happen in parallel.
6. Assign appropriate roles or owners.
7. Define objective completion criteria.
8. Identify risks and likely blockers.
9. Revise the plan when new information changes assumptions.

## Rules

- Prefer the smallest plan that fully satisfies the objective.
- Avoid vague tasks.
- Do not over-plan speculative future work.
- Keep dependencies explicit.
- Make outcomes verifiable.

## Output

Return:
- objective
- approach
- tasks or milestones
- dependencies
- suggested owners
- completion criteria
- risks
