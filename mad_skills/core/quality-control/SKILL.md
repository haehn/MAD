# Quality Control

Use this skill to perform final validation of a deliverable or project output.

## Process

1. Identify required outcomes.
2. Define what can be objectively checked.
3. Validate each critical requirement.
4. Check consistency and completeness.
5. Identify defects or unresolved risks.
6. Record evidence for pass/fail decisions.
7. Escalate unclear or untestable requirements.

## Rules

- Quality claims should be evidence-based.
- Do not hide failures or uncertainty.
- Focus on requirements that materially affect usefulness or correctness.
- Keep validation reproducible when possible.

## Output

Return:
- checks performed
- results
- defects
- unresolved risks
- final status
